Support — Clock Out Angel

Questions, bugs or ideas: wallzengboxer@gmail.com. We usually answer within a few days.

The demon is not on my Lock Screen

Open the app and tap Clock In. Check iOS Settings, Clock Out Angel, Live Activities is on. iOS keeps a Lock Screen card for up to 8 hours; pop a bubble or tap the Clock In widget and a fresh card takes over with all of today's data.

The first pop after a long break is slow

iOS has to wake the app in the background for the first tap. After that it keeps up with fast tapping.

Free trial and purchase

The whole game is free for your first 3 workdays after you tap Start free trial. Only days you clock in count, so weekends and vacations don't use it up. Nothing is charged automatically. After the trial, clocking in needs a one-time unlock; your Demon Dex, albums, wardrobe and vacations stay open either way. If you bought the game and it is locked on a new phone, open Settings in the app, Full game, Restore purchase.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Moving to a new iPhone

Sign in to the same iCloud account on both phones. Your progress follows you.
