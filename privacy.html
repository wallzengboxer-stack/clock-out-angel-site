Privacy Policy — Clock Out Angel

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Clock Out Angel ("the app", "we") is a game for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app handles.

We do not collect your personal information

  • The app does not require an account or login.
  • The app does not send any data to us or to third parties. We run no servers for it.
  • The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking software.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

Your work hours, daily mood counts, reports, collections (demons, angels, postcards), wardrobe, settings and the optional salary figure you may enter are saved on your device. Deleting the app removes them from the device.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app keeps a backup copy of your progress and settings in your own iCloud account (Apple's key-value storage) so it can follow you to a new iPhone. The salary figure is part of your settings. This data is stored by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we cannot see it. You can turn this off in iOS Settings under your Apple Account, iCloud.

Purchases

The free trial and the one-time unlock are handled by Apple's App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your daily and weekly pop counts and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center service so they can appear on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we do not receive your Game Center data. You can play without Game Center.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders (clock in, clock out) are scheduled on your device only.

Sharing

When you tap "Share", the app opens the standard iOS share sheet with an image, video or short code from your game. You choose whether and where to send it. We do not receive it. The app does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

隐私政策（中文）

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 12 日

《牛马日记》（Clock Out Angel）是一款 iPhone 游戏。我们不收集你的个人信息：不需要账号；应用不会向我们或任何第三方发送数据；没有广告、统计或追踪工具。

设备上的数据：上班时间、每天的情绪次数、报告、收集（恶魔、天使、明信片）、装扮、设置，以及你自愿填写的月薪，都保存在你的设备上，删除应用即清除。

iCloud：如果你登录了 iCloud，应用会把进度和设置（含月薪）备份到你自己的 iCloud 账户，方便换手机后继续。数据由苹果按其隐私政策保存，我们看不到。可在系统设置的 iCloud 里关闭。

购买：免费试玩和一次性解锁由苹果 App Store 处理，我们收不到你的姓名、付款信息或 Apple 账户。

Game Center：如果你登录了 Game Center，每日和每周的次数及成就会发送到苹果的 Game Center 服务以显示排行榜，由苹果按其隐私政策处理，我们收不到。

通知和分享：提醒只在你的设备上安排。点"分享"会打开系统分享面板，是否发送由你决定，我们收不到。应用不使用摄像头、麦克风、位置或通讯录。
